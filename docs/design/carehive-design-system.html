The design system

Every screen is assembled from these tokens, so the whole product feels like one warm, considered home.

Palette — warm, pastel, reassuring

Warm Cream#FFF8F0 · ground
Light Beige#F7F4EF · surface
Soft Sage#B7D7C0 · primary
Lavender#DCCEF7 · calm/AI
Soft Peach#FFD7BA · warmth
White Glassrgba · frosted card

Sage is the primary accent (calm, alive, growing). Lavender belongs to the AI companion and moments of calm; peach carries warmth and human touch. Deeper tints (#5E9E7C, #8B76CF, #E0885A) are derived for text/icons so every pairing clears WCAG AA. Semantic colours — good sage warning peach urgent warm coral — stay distinct from the accent, and urgent is a warm coral, never a clinical red.

Typography

Poppins · 800Good morning, Amma
Poppins · 600Today's care
Nunito · bodyWarm, rounded, deeply legible running text that never feels sterile.
Inter · data₹3,999 · 08:00 · 12 Jan 2026

Poppins for display & headings, Nunito for cozy body copy, Inter for numbers, times & data. Base body 17–18px; elder mode scales to 20px+. Line-height ≥1.5, never justified.

Shape, glass & elevation

  • 24px radius on cards, 16px on inputs/chips, pill buttons.
  • Glassmorphism: backdrop-blur(16px), translucent white fill, 1px light border, soft warm shadow — floating, not flat.
  • Soft shadows only (warm-toned, large blur, low opacity) — depth by light, never hard drop-shadows.
  • Floating elements: sticky SOS, floating action buttons, gently layered cards.

Motion & micro-interactions

  • Calm easing (200–320ms), gentle fades & rises — never bouncy or urgent.
  • Page transitions: soft cross-fade + 8px lift. Cards: 2px hover-raise, warm glow.
  • Signature moments: floating particles on splash, breathing AI orb, pulsing SOS ring, confetti-soft check-in.
  • Everything honours prefers-reduced-motion.

Loading & empty states

Skeletons shimmer in the card shape before content arrives:

Empty states are premium, never blank — a soft illustration, a warm line, and one clear action ("Add the parent you care for").

Accessibility — elder-first

  • Touch targets ≥48px (elder mode 56–64px); generous spacing for tremor.
  • Simple Mode: fewer choices, huge tiles, one task per screen.
  • High-contrast & text-scale to 200% without breakage.
  • Voice input & read-aloud on key flows; full screen-reader labels.
  • Colour never the only signal — always icon + label.

Illustration & iconography

  • Warm, multi-generational, real Indian families — soft gouache/flat-vector style, pastel washes.
  • Rounded 2px-stroke icon set; friendly, never medical cross-and-clipboard.
  • Cozy motifs: home, tea, hands, hive, plants, sunrise.
  • Dark mode dims the pastels and deepens the ground to a warm charcoal — still cozy, never cold.
