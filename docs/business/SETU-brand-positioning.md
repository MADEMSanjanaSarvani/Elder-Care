# SETU — Brand Positioning (source of truth)

*The one voice everything speaks in: app, store, pitch, flyer, socials. If copy anywhere
contradicts this page, this page wins.*

---

## The positioning (two lines, always in this order)
> ## SETU — Your parents' safety net.
> *A trusted elder-care ecosystem for families.*

- **Hook (emotional):** "Your parents' safety net." → stops the scroll, hits fear + love.
- **Descriptor (clarity):** "A trusted elder-care ecosystem for families." → explains what it is.

## What SETU is NOT called anymore
- ~~"An AI-powered Elder Care Application"~~ — "AI" is a *feature*, not a benefit; "application" shrinks a service into a download. The AI and the app move **backstage**; the *promise* leads.

## Who we speak to
- **Primary (the payer):** the adult child — often distant/NRI — carrying the guilt of not being there.
- **Beneficiary:** the elder parent.
- **Every line should make the adult child feel: *"With SETU, my parent is safe, and I can breathe."***

## Approved lines (mix + match by channel)
- **Master tagline:** *Your parents' safety net.*
- **Descriptor:** *A trusted elder-care ecosystem for families.*
- **NRI / distance angle:** *Because you can't always be there.*
- **Dual benefit:** *Peace of mind for you. A safety net for them.*
- **Trust proof one-liner:** *Verified caregivers. Daily updates. One-tap emergency help.*

## Say / Don't say
| Say (benefit + feeling) | Don't say (feature + tech) |
|---|---|
| "Your parents' safety net" | "AI-powered elder care app" |
| "You always know they're safe" | "Real-time data platform" |
| "Verified, trained caregivers" | "Caregiver marketplace SaaS" |
| "One-tap emergency help (108-first)" | "Integrated SOS module" |
| "Daily updates, so you can breathe" | "Automated timeline feed" |

## The promise discipline (important)
"Safety net" is a promise to *catch them when they fall* — powerful **and** demanding. Never
advertise a guaranteed emergency response time you can't uphold; always keep the **108-first**
framing (SETU coordinates + alerts; it is not an emergency service). Earn the word with
verification, reliability, and honest disclaimers — a safety net that fails once is worse than
never claiming to be one.

## Where this is already applied
- App **login screen** subtitle → hook + descriptor.
- **Play Store listing** (`docs/store/STORE-LISTING.md`) → short + full description lead with the hook.
- Use it next on: feature graphic, screenshots captions, flyer, Instagram/LinkedIn bio, pitch deck cover.

*The tech (AI, app, charts, Agora, Razorpay) is HOW we keep the promise. The promise — a safety
net for someone's parents — is WHAT we sell. Never confuse the two again.*
