This Privacy Policy explains how [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us", "Setu") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use the Setu family & elder app and the Setu Care caregiver app (together, the "Apps"). It is written to align with the DPDP Act, 2023.

1. Who this applies to

  • Elders who receive care through Setu.
  • Family members linked to an elder's account.
  • Caregivers who provide services through Setu Care.

Where an elder cannot consent for themselves, a family member or guardian may act on a documented basis as permitted by law (see Section 8).

2. What data we collect

You give us: - Account & identity: phone number (used to sign in), display name, preferred language, and — for elders — date of birth. - Health information (sensitive): blood type, allergies, chronic conditions, current medications, and emergency medical notes, where you choose to add them. - Care information: bookings, visit activity logs, caregiver visit notes, daily well-being check-ins, appointments, and reminders. - Caregivers additionally provide: identity and verification documents (government ID, police verification, professional council certificate, insurance) and payout bank/UPI details.

Collected as you use the Apps: - Location — only at the moment you trigger the SOS/emergency flow, to share the elder's location with family and our on-call team. We do not track location in the background. - Usage & audit records — a log of who accessed which care records and when, which we keep to protect the elder and to power the in-app "who has seen this data" view.

3. Why we use it (purposes)

  • To provide the care service: bookings, visits, reminders, medication management, appointments, and the care timeline.
  • To run the emergency (SOS) feature and alert family and on-call staff.
  • To operate consent-based sharing between an elder and their family.
  • To verify caregivers and keep the platform safe.
  • To process payments and caregiver payouts.
  • To provide optional AI features (a care assistant, visit summaries, translations) — see Section 5.
  • To meet legal, tax, and safety obligations.

We rely on your consent for sensitive (health) data and, where applicable, on the legitimate uses permitted by the DPDP Act (such as responding to a medical emergency).

4. Consent and your control

  • An elder controls, per category (e.g. live location, health notes, medication list, billing), what each linked family member can see, and can revoke any of it at any time in the app.
  • Adding sensitive health information is optional; you choose what to enter.
  • During a genuine medical emergency, essential health information may be shared with responders and on-call staff to protect the elder's life, consistent with the DPDP Act's provisions for medical emergencies.

5. AI features (optional)

If enabled, the AI care assistant, visit summaries, and translation send only the specific operational text needed to a third-party AI provider ([AI PROVIDER, e.g. OpenAI]) to produce a response. These features: - never make medical diagnoses or medication decisions; - run behind safety filters, and any output that could contain medical advice is withheld pending human review; - are off unless we have configured them.

If you do not want AI processing, do not use the AI assistant; the rest of the Apps work without it.

  • Data is stored on [Supabase, region: ____]. Any cross-border transfer is made only as permitted under the DPDP Act.
  • We keep personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise it, unless a longer period is required by law (e.g. tax/financial records).

8. Your rights under the DPDP Act

You can: - Access a copy of your data (the app provides a data-export option). - Correct or update your information. - Erase your data / withdraw consent (the app provides an erasure-request option); some records may be retained where law requires. - Nominate another person to exercise your rights (e.g. in case of incapacity or death), as provided by the DPDP Act. - Grieve — raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (Section 11), and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved.

9. Children and guardianship

Setu is intended for adults. Care is provided to elders; where an elder is unable to consent, a family member or guardian may act on a documented basis. We do not knowingly create accounts for persons under 18.

10. How we protect data

Access to care records is controlled by row-level security tied to consent, and every access to sensitive records is logged. Documents are held in private storage reachable only through short-lived, permission-checked links. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.
