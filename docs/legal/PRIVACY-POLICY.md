This Privacy Policy explains how **[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]** ("we", "us",
"CareHive") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you use the
CareHive app (the "App"). It is written to align with the DPDP Act, 2023.

## 1. Who this applies to

- **Elders** whose medicine record is kept in CareHive.
- **Family members** linked to an elder's account.

Where an elder cannot consent for themselves, a family member or guardian may
act on a **documented basis** as permitted by law (see Section 7).

## 2. What data we collect

**You give us:**
- **Account & identity:** email address and/or phone number (used to sign in),
  display name, preferred language, region, and — for elders — date of birth.
- **Health information (sensitive):** medicines and their dose times, blood
  type, allergies, chronic conditions, emergency medical notes, and any medical
  documents you upload, where you choose to add them.
- **The medicine record:** for each scheduled dose, whether it was marked
  taken or not taken, the time of the mark, and who made it. Doses that were
  never marked are stored as such and are not inferred either way.
- **Care information:** daily well-being check-ins, appointments, hospital
  stays and reminders.

**Collected as you use the App:**
- **A notification token**, so a reminder or an alert can reach the device.
- **Location** — only at the moment you trigger the **SOS/emergency** flow, to
  share the elder's location with the family. We do not track location in the
  background, and we do not read it at any other time.
- **Usage & audit records** — a log of who accessed which records and when,
  which we keep to protect the elder and to power the in-app "who has seen this
  data" view.

**We do not collect:** payment or financial information of any kind. CareHive
is free and takes no payments.

## 3. Why we use it (purposes)

- To remind you at each dose time. (The alarm itself is scheduled on your own
  device and fires without sending anything to us.)
- To keep the medicine record, so it can be shown to a doctor or read in an
  emergency.
- To run the **emergency (SOS)** feature and alert the family.
- To operate **consent-based sharing** between an elder and their family.
- To meet legal and safety obligations.

We rely on your **consent** for sensitive (health) data and, where applicable,
on the legitimate uses permitted by the DPDP Act (such as responding to a
medical emergency).

## 4. Consent and your control

- An elder controls, **per category** (e.g. medication list, health notes,
  location, visit history), what each linked family member can see, and can
  **revoke** any of it at any time in the app.
- Adding sensitive health information is optional; you choose what to enter.
- During a genuine medical emergency, essential health information may be
  shared with responders and family to protect the elder's life, consistent
  with the DPDP Act's provisions for medical emergencies.

## 5. Who we share data with (processors)

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. There is no
advertising in CareHive. We share data only with service providers that help us
run it, under contract and only for the purposes above:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **[Supabase]** | Secure hosting, database, and file storage |
| **[Google Firebase Cloud Messaging]** | Delivering emergency and family notifications |
| **[SMS/email provider]** | Sending your one-time login code |

We may also disclose data where required by law or to protect someone's safety.

## 6. Where data is stored and for how long

- Data is stored on **[Supabase, region: ____]**. Any cross-border transfer is
  made only as permitted under the DPDP Act.
- We keep personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed for
  the purposes above, then delete or anonymise it, unless a longer period is
  required by law.

## 7. Your rights under the DPDP Act

You can:
- **Access** a copy of your data (the app provides a data-export option).
- **Correct** or update your information.
- **Erase** your data / withdraw consent (the app provides an erasure-request
  option); some records may be retained where law requires.
- **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights (e.g. in case of
  incapacity or death), as provided by the DPDP Act.
- **Grieve** — raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer (Section 10), and
  escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved.

## 8. Children and guardianship

CareHive is intended for adults. Where an elder is unable to consent, a family
member or guardian may act on a documented basis. We do not knowingly create
accounts for persons under 18.

## 9. How we protect data

Access to records is controlled by row-level security tied to consent, and
every access to sensitive records is logged. Documents are held in private
storage reachable only through short-lived, permission-checked links. Data is
encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable
technical and organisational measures to protect your data.
